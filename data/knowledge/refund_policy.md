# Customer Refund Policy

## Refund Eligibility

A refund may be proposed when a verified billing discrepancy
caused the customer to pay more than the authorized amount.

The refundable amount must not exceed the verified
overpayment.

## Approval Requirement

Refunds greater than 100 monetary units require human
approval before execution.

The investigation system may recommend a refund but must not
execute an approval-required refund automatically.
