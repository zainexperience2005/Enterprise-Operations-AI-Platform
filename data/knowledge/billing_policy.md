# Enterprise Billing Policy

Policy ID: BILL-001
Version: 1.0

## Invoice Accuracy

All customer invoices must correspond to the approved total
of the associated customer order.

Additional charges may only appear when they represent an
approved tax, shipping fee, service charge, or documented
post-order adjustment.

If an invoice exceeds the approved order total and no
authorized adjustment exists, the difference must be treated
as a billing discrepancy.

## Billing Discrepancy Review

When a billing discrepancy is reported, operations staff must
verify:

1. the original order total;
2. the issued invoice amount;
3. completed payments;
4. any approved adjustments.

A correction must not be executed until the discrepancy has
been verified.
