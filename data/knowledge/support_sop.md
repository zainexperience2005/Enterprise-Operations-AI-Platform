# Customer Support Billing SOP

Policy ID: SUP-001
Version: 1.0

## Billing Complaints

When a customer reports an incorrect invoice, support staff
must create or update a support ticket describing the
reported discrepancy.

The investigation should collect evidence from the order,
invoice, payment records, and applicable billing policy.

Support staff must not promise a refund before eligibility
and required approval have been confirmed.
